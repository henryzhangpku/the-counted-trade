# 17. Toss a Brick to Attract Jade

*Part III: Stratagems for Attack*

1. The brick is thrown to bring back jade. It is worth little, and that is precisely why it can be thrown.

2. A candidate is a brick. Most are discarded, and the discarding is the work.

3. Thousands come in: every name with a chain, every date on the calendar, every story with a price.

4. The gates stand in order: liquidity, a dated catalyst, volatility, structure, risk, edge. Each gate removes, and none is skipped.

5. Liquidity first, because nothing else can be priced without it. Edge last, because it is the one that fails quietly.

6. Five to ten leave the funnel, at most. Some weeks none leave, and the funnel is still working.

7. A funnel that passes everything has no gates. A funnel that passes nothing has no candidates.

8. The jade is not the number of ideas. It is the one that survives every gate and still looks cheap.

## Commentary

The funnel is the plainest machine in this book, and the one most often abandoned. It begins with everything the calendar and the chain can offer, and it ends with a short list. Between the two are six filters applied in a fixed order, so that the same candidate meets the same questions every week. A gate skipped once is skipped every week thereafter.

The order matters. Liquidity is first because a candidate that cannot be traded cannot be evaluated; a beautiful thesis in a dead strike is a story, not a trade. The dated catalyst comes next, because a buyer of options is paying for time and needs to know what the time is for. Volatility, structure and risk narrow the expression. Edge is last, and it is last on purpose: it is the filter most easily fooled by a good narrative, and the one applied to a candidate that has already survived the cheaper tests.

Five to ten names at the end is a full week's work, and often the honest number is zero. The funnel is not failing when it produces nothing. It is failing when it produces fifteen, because that means a gate was left open. The list is not a watchlist. It is the residue of a test, and it is allowed to be empty.
