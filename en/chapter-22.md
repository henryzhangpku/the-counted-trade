# 22. Shut the Door to Catch the Thief

*Part IV: Stratagems for Confused Situations*

1. When the thief is in the house, shut the door. Do not chase him into the fields, where the ground knows him better than it knows you.

2. The plan is the door, and it shuts at entry. After that the thief is inside terms already written.

3. Do not move the stop because the price came close. Do not widen the spread because the debit became uncomfortable. Do not extend the time stop because the thesis is "still intact".

4. "Give it room" is the thief's phrase, spoken in the trader's voice.

5. Tightening is permitted. A smaller loss is still a loss taken by plan. Loosening is the theft.

6. A plan that may be revised under loss is not a plan. It is a preference with a schedule.

7. The door has one hinge: the maximum loss was fixed before entry, and nothing after entry is new information about it. News is information about the thesis, not about the risk.

8. A thief caught in a shut room is a cost. A thief let into the fields is a career.

## Commentary

The stratagem is about containment. A thief inside a closed room can be dealt with at leisure; a thief who reaches open ground must be chased on his own terms. The trader's open ground is the space between the stop he wrote and the stop he is now inventing. Every step into it is a step away from the counted trade and toward a position whose worst case is unknown again.

The three forbidden moves are the same move. Moving the stop, widening the spread, extending the time stop — each takes a loss that was measured at entry and makes it unmeasured at the worst possible moment, when the position is already losing. They rarely arrive as a decision. They arrive as a sentence: the thesis needs one more week, the strike was too close, the earnings date slipped. The sentence is always reasonable. That is what makes it the thief.

Tightening is the one revision allowed, because it cannot enlarge the worst case. A stop brought closer, a spread closed early, a time stop honoured — these are still acts of the plan, made in the plan's direction. But the door has to be shut once, at entry, or the room has no walls at all.
