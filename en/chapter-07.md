# 7. Create Something From Nothing

*Part II: Stratagems for Confronting the Enemy*

1. Straw men went over the wall at night. The besiegers loosed at them until the quivers were empty, and by dawn the garrison had arrows. The next night the real men came down.

2. A backtest works the same way. Turn a filter, move a threshold, and a dead rule stands up in the data wearing the shape of an edge.

3. Nothing was found. Something was made. The curve that only rises at the tenth variation is a straw man at dusk.

4. The market is not a wall of archers. It has seen the figure before, and it prices the figure, not the man.

5. Therefore write before you look: the rule, the instrument, the entry, the exits, the size, and the test that would prove the rule false. Date the page. Do not edit it.

6. A rule written after the results is not a rule. It is a caption.

7. Count the trials. Ten filters tried and one kept is one kept out of ten, and the kept one carries the luck of the nine discarded.

8. The pass line must be a number set in advance: this sample, this return, this drawdown, or the idea dies.

9. Pre-registration does not create an edge. It makes the absence of one visible, which is worth more than most edges.

10. He who can kill his own rule on the day the test fails has gained what the rule could never give him: a method.

## Commentary

A backtest is a mirror, not a window. The researcher who tries twenty variations and keeps the one that worked has not tested twenty ideas. He has tested one idea twenty times and reported the luckiest. The reported result is conditioned on the search, and the search is invisible in the equity curve.

The cure is procedural rather than moral. One page, dated before the first run: the universe, the rule, the structure, the exits, the size, the sample window, and the result that counts as failure. After that, no edits. If the rule needs a filter added after the loss appeared, the filter is part of the result, and the test must begin again with the trial count raised.

Two habits make the page honest. Report the search, not only the winner: how many variations, how many symbols, how many windows. And keep a final period untouched until the question is settled, so the arbiter has not already read the argument.

A pre-registered test that fails is not a wasted month. It is the only kind of test that can teach anything, because it was capable of failing. The alternative, a rule that was always going to look good, teaches only that the curve can be made to rise.
